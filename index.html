Flying safely

Every flight shown here follows India's Drone Rules, 2021.

Drones over 250 g need a Unique Identification Number (UIN) on Digital Sky, and their pilot a Remote Pilot Certificate from a DGCA-authorised school. Always check the Digital Sky airspace map (green, yellow, red zones) before you fly. Build notes on this site are for learning; they are not a certification that a design is safe to fly.
